# Qualihome — Concerns and Observations
**Date:** September 14, 2026

---

## Issue 1: Recommended Houses — Qualification Filter Bug

**Description:**
Houses that are **not qualified** across all loan terms (20/15/10/5 years) should not appear in the "Recommended Houses" section. A house should only appear if the client is at least **Conditionally Qualified** for at least one year term.

**Expected Behavior:**
- Only show houses with a status of **QUALIFIED** or **CONDITIONAL QUALIFIED**.
- Do **not** show houses that are **NOT QUALIFIED** across all years — even if the client set a minimum and maximum budget in their assessment.

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Complete a pre-qualification assessment with a set budget range.
2. Go to "Recommended Houses."
3. Houses marked as "Not Qualified" for all loan terms (20/15/10/5 yrs) are still appearing.

## Issue 2: Forgot Password — Reset Email Not Sending

## Issue 3: Number Inputs — Missing Comma Formatting

**Description:**
Number input fields do not display comma separators, making large values hard to read during the pre-qualification assessment.

**Affected Fields (examples):**
- Gross Monthly Income (₱)
- Monthly Debt / Loan Payments (₱)

**Example:**
- Current display: `100000.00`, `42145`
- Expected display: `100,000.00`, `42,145`

**Request:** Add comma formatting to all number input fields throughout the app.

---

## Issue 4: Pre-Qualification Assessment — No Field for Number of Dependents

**Description:**
The pre-qualification assessment (Step 2: Employment) does not include a field for **Number of Dependents**, even though this information is factored into **Conditional Qualification** results.

**Affected Step:** Step 2 — Employment Details

**Request:** Add a "Number of Dependents" input field in the Employment step of the pre-qualification assessment.

---

## Issue 5: House Cards — Remove "High Similarity" Indicator

**Description:**
House listing cards currently display a **"High Similarity"** badge/indicator. This should be removed.

**Affected Area:** House listing cards (visible in Recommended Houses and search results)

**Request:** Remove the "High Similarity" label/indicator from all house cards.

## Issue 6: Money Inputs — Values Disappear on 4th Digit (Regression from Comma Formatting)

**Description:**
On Register, money fields clear themselves as soon as a 4th digit is typed. Affects Financial Capability (Gross Monthly Income) and Preferred Model (Optional) & Budget (Minimum Budget, Maximum Budget) — reportedly "halos lahat ng may about sa pera, nag gaganon".

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Go to Register (Get Qualified wizard).
2. In Step 2 → Financial Capability → Gross Monthly Income, type 4 digits (e.g. `1000`).
3. On the 4th digit the numbers disappear (same on Minimum/Maximum Budget in Step 3).

**Expected Behavior:**
- Typing `1000` should show `1,000` and keep the value editable/submittable.
- Same for all money fields in Register and the dashboard re-assessment wizard.
